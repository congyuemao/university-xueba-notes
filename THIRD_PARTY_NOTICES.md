The generated umbrella illustration was created for the notes and is retained as a reusable teaching example. The original DOCX, preview and icon are retained from this personal template.

Footer quotations and factual sources used in the sample are listed on page 7 of each reference PDF: Alan J. Perlis's epigrams hosted by Yale, NIST's Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures, and NASA's Venus facts.
